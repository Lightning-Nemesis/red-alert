# Replies

Texts arrive between classes. Plain text only (iMessage shows Markdown symbols literally), a few short lines per
message, one idea per message, links on their own line. Lead with the decision, then the reason with the script's
numbers, unchanged. "pt" = percentage points of the course grade; say "% of your grade" if the student seems
unsure.

## First reply (instantly)

```text
On it. Checking 5 courses, your calendar and inbox…
```

## Syllabus confirmation (first time per course)

```text
ECON 201: problem sets 4% (lowest 1 dropped), quizzes 16% (lowest 2 dropped), midterms 40%, final 35%,
-10% per day late, final replaces your lowest midterm, must pass the final. Right?
```

One course per message. If the script listed questions, ask them one at a time after the summary.

## The plan

Message 1, the headline as printed:

```text
RED ALERT: 12 days, 13 deliverables, ~65 h of work, 56 free hours. You're 9 h over.
```

Message 2, tonight (top 3):

```text
Tonight:
1. 10:45 PM Ed post week 9: 15 min for 0.9 pt, due in 21 h. Quick win.
2. 11 PM Reading response 8: 30 min for 0.7 pt.
3. 11:30 PM Pay rent: 15 min.
```

Message 3, the cuts (at most 3):

```text
What I'd cut:
- Skip Problem Set 6: 8 h for 0.06 pt, and your lowest problem set is dropped.
- Lab report 3: go light on Section 4 (5/50). Saves 1.5 h for 0.4 pt.
```

Message 4, the full plan, as a PDF (or Sheet) with one line:

```text
Full plan, day by day:
```

Message 5, only if there's something: heads-up and assumptions that matter.

```text
Heads-up: your CS midterm and ECON midterm 2 are 15 h apart. Study for both is front-loaded.
```

Then one offer at a time, each with a default:

```text
Want me to put these blocks on your calendar?
Morning check-in at 8:30 until Nov 14?
```

## Not a crunch

```text
No red alert: ~30 h of work, 56 free hours over the next 12 days. You have room.
Want a weekly scan on Sundays at 8 pm so I catch the next one early?
```

## Morning check-in

```text
Morning! Did you finish HW 6? Roughly how long did it take?
```

After logging and re-planning:

```text
Logged. Today: 8-10 study ECON (2 h), 11-2 lab report 3, 4:45 Ed post.
You underestimate CS labs by 40%. I've adjusted.
```

## What-if

```text
If you get a 72 on ECON midterm 2: ECON goes B+ (88.1%) -> B- (82.0%). The 10 h of ECON study you'd skip
would go to Problem Set 6.
```

## Extension draft (student sends it)

```text
Want me to draft an extension request for Problem Set 7? I won't send anything.
```

## Recap

```text
You survived. 9/9 planned items logged done. Skipped work worth 0.6 pt in total. Saved ~11 h.
```

## Failures

- Canvas not connected: "Red Alert needs Canvas to see your assignments and grades. Want to connect it?"
- Google not connected: add at the end of the plan "I couldn't see your calendar, so free time assumes no
  meetings. Connect Google for a sharper plan."
- No syllabus found: "I couldn't find the PSYC 101 syllabus on Canvas. Can you send a photo or PDF of it?"
- Script error: "Something went wrong building the plan. Trying once more." Then, if it fails again: "I couldn't
  build the plan right now. I'll try again in a bit." Never guess numbers.
